# Manual outreach sequence

The sequence is a small, human-reviewed cadence. It is not a scheduler and it never sends automatically.

## Current phase override — informational batch

For the first authorized batch, use [FIRST-INFORMATIONAL-OUTREACH.md](./FIRST-INFORMATIONAL-OUTREACH.md). The Day 2/3 and Day 6/7 examples below are **superseded for this phase**: do not follow up until the natural response rate of the first batch has been measured.

## Day 0 — first contact

Use one approved WhatsApp, Instagram DM or email variant, chosen for the verified public channel. One business, one channel, one CTA. Record timestamp, copy, source, owner type and reviewer.

## Day 2/3 — one follow-up

> Hola, [NOMBRE]. Retomo esto una sola vez por si te interesaba ver Austral. Si ahora no es prioridad, no hay problema y cierro el seguimiento.

Use only when there is no reply, no bounce, no DNC and no identity concern.

## Day 6/7 — last contact

> Hola, [NOMBRE]. Cierro el seguimiento para no insistir de más. Si más adelante querés revisar agenda o reservas, podés escribirme por acá.

This is the final attempt. Do not add a second CTA or a new channel.

## STOP conditions

Stop immediately after any response, including a question, “ahora no”, a complaint, a request for later, a bounce, a DNC request or an identity concern. Classify the response and route it to Lautaro.

Never contact a lead marked `DO_NOT_CONTACT`, `NOT_INTERESTED`, `INVALID`, duplicate, `AGENCY`, `SOFTWARE_PROVIDER` or unresolved `UNKNOWN`.

## Tracking

Record `first_contact_date`, `followup_date`, channel, exact message, delivery/bounce, response classification, reviewer and next action in the CRM source of truth. Use the empty tracker template only for preparation; do not maintain a parallel permanent CRM.
