# Value proposition

## Why a barbería would try Austral

Austral helps a barbería keep agenda, reservations, clients, services, professionals and schedules in one clearer operating flow. The first value is less manual coordination and fewer decisions lost in memory or scattered chats.

## Practical outcomes to test

- Fewer repeated messages about availability.
- A more orderly agenda for the owner and team.
- Easier public reservations when the configured availability is correct.
- Services, professionals and schedules visible in the same workspace.
- Client information organized with the business's permission.
- Less dependence on paper notes, memory and disconnected chats.

These are hypotheses to validate during a 15-day pilot, not guaranteed outcomes.

## What Austral is not promising

- Guaranteed higher revenue.
- Guaranteed reduction in no-shows.
- Perfect AI or autonomous operation.
- General-purpose autonomous WhatsApp automation.
- Automatic billing or payment collection.
- Plan entitlements that have not been defined.

## Honest explanation

“Austral busca ordenar la agenda, las reservas, los servicios, los profesionales y los clientes de una barbería en un solo lugar. Lo probamos 15 días con tu operación real y vemos si te ahorra trabajo.”

WhatsApp is an integration under progressive validation. Describe only the scope that is currently confirmed for the pilot.
