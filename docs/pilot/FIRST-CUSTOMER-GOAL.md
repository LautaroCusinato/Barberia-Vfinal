# First customer goal

## Focus

The first objective is **one real customer using Austral**, not 100 accounts or maximum outreach volume.

After the first validated customer, aim for three. Only then optimize acquisition or expand the cohort.

## Milestones

1. `FIRST RESPONSE` — a qualified business replies.
2. `FIRST DEMO` — the owner or responsible employee sees the real scope.
3. `FIRST TRIAL` — a business consents to a 15-day pilot and onboarding is complete.
4. `FIRST REAL BOOKING` — the business manages a real reservation through the approved flow.
5. `FIRST PAYING CLIENT` — continuity is accepted and handled manually.

## Evidence for each milestone

Record date, business, responsible contact, channel, next action and the smallest useful note in the CRM source of truth. Do not count a sent message as success. Do not count a demo request as a trial. Do not count a trial as a paying customer.

## Stop conditions

Pause expansion if replies show confusion about scope, a repeated product gap, a privacy/DNC concern, a P0/P1 issue or a promise that the product cannot currently honor. Review the message and product hypothesis with Lautaro before continuing.
