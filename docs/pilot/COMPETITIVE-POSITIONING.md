# Competitive positioning

This is a workflow comparison, not an attack on other tools. Do not state competitor pricing, features or migration claims without current evidence.

| Current approach | Strength | Common gap Austral may test |
|---|---|---|
| Paper agenda | Familiar and immediate | Availability, team coordination and history are hard to share |
| Google Calendar | Flexible general calendar | Services, professionals, client context and public booking are not one barbería workflow |
| Manual WhatsApp | Personal and widely adopted | Repeated questions and scattered confirmations consume owner time |
| Simple booking page | Fast public reservation | May not cover team, services, clients, breaks or operational context together |
| Large booking app | More mature feature set | Can be heavier than needed for a small owner-led barbería |

## Austral's possible advantage

Austral is positioned as a focused operational workspace for barberías: enough structure to coordinate agenda, services, professionals, clients and public reservations without asking a small team to start with an enterprise process.

## How to speak about an existing system

“Perfecto, no buscamos reemplazar algo que ya funciona sin entenderlo. Podemos mirar sólo el flujo que hoy les cuesta más y ver si Austral aporta algo.”

Never imply that Austral is cheaper, better or more automated than a named competitor unless the comparison has been verified for the current pilot.

## Pricing posture during the pilot

Austral se presenta con un único plan de ARS 50.000/mes y 15 días gratis. Si el negocio tiene más de una sucursal, responder “lo vemos según el caso”. No comparar precios, ofrecer descuentos ni inventar un nivel enterprise.
