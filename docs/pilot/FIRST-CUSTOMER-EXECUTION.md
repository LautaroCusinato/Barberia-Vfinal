# First-customer execution plan

This plan is deliberately small: learn from a few conversations before expanding. The CRM remains the source of truth; [PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv](./PROSPECT-TRACKER.csv) is an empty preparation template, not a second CRM.

The current first-contact phase is informational: use [FIRST-INFORMATIONAL-OUTREACH.md](./FIRST-INFORMATIONAL-OUTREACH.md), wait for natural replies and do not offer access, links, immediate demo, trial or payment.

## Sequence

1. Prepare 20 qualified prospects with public evidence and identity classification.
2. Select the top 10 by score, fit and low switching friction.
3. Human-review and contact the first 5 manually, one business at a time.
4. Measure delivery, replies, objections, demo requests and DNC.
5. Adjust the copy only from observed evidence.
6. Human-review and contact the second 5.
7. Run demos for qualified responders.
8. Offer a 15-day trial only after scope, consent and onboarding readiness are clear.
9. Seek the first real booking and then manual paid continuity.

## Cohort gates

Pause after the first five if there is a repeated identity issue, DNC concern, copy confusion, product limitation or a P0/P1 problem. Do not complete the next five automatically.

## Success measures

- Response quality, not send volume.
- Demo completed with the owner or responsible employee.
- Trial started with explicit consent and correct workspace.
- First real booking managed through the approved flow.
- Paid continuation discussed manually with no invented entitlements.

## Channel order

Use the verified channel the business actually publishes. Prefer a direct commercial WhatsApp or email for a small owner-led barbería; use Instagram DM only when the business account is the clear commercial channel. Never switch channels to bypass a non-response or DNC.
